Why North-Eastern Region (NER) India is Highly Vulnerable

The North-Eastern Himalayan Region features high seismicity, fragile tertiary sedimentary lithology (shale, phyllite), steep fault scarps, and world-record monsoon precipitation (Cherrapunji & Mawsynram). During heavy downpours, pore-water pressure rapidly destabilizes mountain slopes. LandSafe AI provides early warning intelligence to safeguard communities, mountain highways, and emergency responders.

How LandSafe AI Operates

Step 1: Telemetry Ingestion

Retrieves real-time atmospheric data from Open-Meteo and digital elevation models with geographic coordinate spacing.

Step 2: 3D Topographic Modeling

Executes Horn 3×3 gradient algorithm over local elevation grids to compute precise slope angles in degrees.

Step 3: Random Forest Inference

Processes antecedent moisture and geotechnical indices to predict risk category, class probabilities, and dominant triggers.

Step 4: Proactive Alerts

Dispatches multi-channel early warnings, speed limit caution notifications, and pre-formatted emergency SOS transmissions.

Meteorological Context for Landslide Occurrence

Rainfall is the paramount trigger for mass-wasting events in the North-Eastern Himalayas. Continuous downpours saturate the upper soil mantle, increasing unit weight while dissipating matric suction. Once antecedent moisture reaches field capacity, pore-water pressure drastically decreases shear resistance along planar slip surfaces.

1. Rotational Slump (घूर्णी भूस्खलन)

Mass of soil and weathered rock slips along a spoon-shaped concave curved surface. Common in tertiary sedimentary shale and siltstone slopes of Meghalaya and Mizoram.

🌊

2. Debris Flow & Mudslide (मलबे का प्रवाह)

Rapid movement of water-saturated debris and mud down steep gullies reaching speeds over 40 km/h. Triggered by extreme cloudbursts exceeding 60 mm/hour.

🧱

3. Structural Mitigation (इंजीनियरिंग रोकथाम)

Installation of perforated horizontal sub-drains, shotcrete with soil nails, gabion retaining toe walls, and vetiver grass bio-engineering to restore shear strength.

Official Landslide Safety Checklist

Before (Early Warning Phase)

  • Inspect surrounding slopes for tension cracks or sudden muddy seepage.
  • Keep emergency go-bag ready with drinking water, dry rations, and torch.
  • Know multiple evacuation routes away from steep gullies and drainage paths.

During (Slope Failure In Progress)

  • Move away from the path of debris flow or landslide as rapidly as possible.
  • Never seek shelter inside culverts, stream beds, or beneath retaining walls.
  • If trapped indoors, curl into a tight ball and protect your head under sturdy furniture.
